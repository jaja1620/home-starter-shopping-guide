# 入住前 7 天待办 / Pre-Move-In 7-Day Timeline

从 D-7 到 D+1，每天只需处理 2–3 件事。顺序不能乱：验收与预约要最先做，燃气与宽带决定能不能住，保洁必须在大件进场前完成。

From D-7 to D+1 there are only two or three tasks per day. The order matters: inspection and booking come first, gas and broadband decide whether you can actually live there, and deep cleaning must finish before the big items arrive.

---

## D-7 · 验收与预约 Inspection and Booking

**中文**：带卷尺、手电与手机逐间验收：墙面空鼓、窗缝渗水、地漏是否返味、配电箱空开是否齐全、每个插座是否通电。同时打三个电话：开荒保洁（确认含窗户、油烟机、地漏，约到场时间）、品牌售后（冰箱、洗衣机、烟机灶具、热水器、空调逐项报装）、宽带运营商（确认可装带宽与上门日期）。今天不做，后面所有环节都会顺延。

**English**: Walk through every room with a tape measure, torch and phone: check for hollow plaster, window leaks, smelly floor drains, a complete breaker panel and live sockets. Then make three calls — deep-cleaning crew (confirm windows, range hood and floor drains are included and agree on a slot), brand service lines (book installation for refrigerator, washing machine, hood-hob set, water heater and air conditioners one by one) and the broadband provider (confirm available speed and an on-site date). Skip today and everything downstream slips.

---

## D-6 · 水电燃气过户与宽带报装 Utilities Transfer and Broadband

**中文**：办理水、电、燃气过户或新开，拍照记录水电表底数，避免替上一任住户的欠费买单。宽带当天报装，一般 1–3 天上门；路由器位置定在房屋中心，别塞进弱电箱金属柜。同步确认物业的装修/搬运时间窗口与货梯预约规则。

**English**: Transfer or open water, electricity and gas accounts, and photograph the meter readings so you do not pay the previous occupant's arrears. Order broadband today — installation usually takes one to three days; place the router centrally, not inside a metal cabinet. Also confirm the property management's moving time windows and goods-lift booking rules.

---

## D-5 · 燃气通气点火 Gas Commissioning

**中文**：燃气公司上门点火通气，需本人在场并携带身份证与房产/租房凭证。当场测试灶具点火、热水器出水温度与烟道排烟，检查是否装止逆阀、软管是否为不锈钢波纹管。通气后记录燃气表读数与报修电话。

**English**: The gas utility visits to commission the supply; you must be present with your ID and property or lease documents. Test hob ignition, water-heater outlet temperature and flue exhaust on the spot, and check that a backflow damper is fitted and the hose is stainless corrugated. Record the meter reading and the service hotline afterwards.

---

## D-4 · 通风散味与甲醛检测 Ventilation and Formaldehyde Test

**中文**：开窗形成对流，柜门与抽屉全部打开，床垫与沙发靠墙立起留缝。如做了定制柜或新家具，安排甲醛检测（关闭门窗 12 小时后采样更接近真实居住条件）。当天不安排任何安装，给味道留出时间。

**English**: Create cross-ventilation, open every cabinet door and drawer, and stand mattresses and sofas away from the wall. If you have custom cabinets or new furniture, schedule a formaldehyde test — sampling after 12 hours with doors and windows closed better reflects real living conditions. Book no installations today; let the smell dissipate.

---

## D-3 · 开荒保洁 Deep Cleaning

**中文**：开荒保洁进场，重点：窗框与窗轨、油烟机内部、地漏与下水、踢脚线与门套上沿、灯具与开关面板。保洁完成后 24 小时内不要开柜门，避免灰尘二次落回。当天把"生存包"送到新家：床品四件套、洗漱用品、纸巾、拖鞋、烧水壶、两套换洗衣物。

**English**: The deep-cleaning crew comes in and focuses on window frames and tracks, the inside of the range hood, floor drains and waste pipes, skirting boards and door-head trims, light fittings and switch plates. Do not open cabinets for 24 hours afterwards or dust settles back in. Deliver the "survival kit" today: bedding set, toiletries, tissues, slippers, kettle and two changes of clothes.

---

## D-2 · 大件进场与厨卫试水 Bulky Delivery and Wet Testing

**中文**：家电与家具集中进场，先大后小、先重后轻，冰箱与洗衣机搬入后静置 2 小时以上再通电。厨房与卫生间同步试水：所有龙头开至最大看是否渗漏、地漏倒水试排水速度、马桶连续冲水三次查返味。发现渗漏立刻联系物业关总阀。

**English**: Appliances and furniture arrive together — big before small, heavy before light — and the refrigerator and washing machine should stand for at least two hours before powering up. Test water in kitchen and bathroom at the same time: run every tap fully to check for leaks, pour water down the drains to gauge flow, and flush the toilet three times to check for odours. If a leak appears, ask property management to shut the main valve immediately.

---

## D-1 · 通电试机与安装收尾 Power-On Testing and Installation Close-Out

**中文**：冰箱空载运行 2 小时后放第一批食物；洗衣机先空洗一次（加少量洗涤剂）再洗衣服；热水器连续放水 5 分钟测恒温；空调每台试机 30 分钟以上，确认制冷、排水与噪音；烟机开最大档听噪音并检查是否倒灌。全部安装工单、发票与保修卡归档进 `docs/warranty-register.md`。

**English**: Run the refrigerator empty for two hours, then load the first batch of food. Run the washing machine through one empty cycle with a little detergent before the first wash. Run the water heater for five minutes to check temperature stability. Test each air conditioner for at least 30 minutes for cooling, drainage and noise, and run the hood on maximum to check noise and backflow. File every installation ticket, invoice and warranty card into `docs/warranty-register.md`.

---

## D+1 · 开箱归位 Unpacking and Settling In

**中文**：按"生存包"逻辑归位：先卧室（床架、床垫、四件套、窗帘）保证当晚能睡；再厨房（锅具、餐具、调味、清洁耗材）保证能开火；最后客厅（沙发、茶几、电视、路由）。当天只做归位不做采购，缺什么记在手机上，第二天集中补。

**English**: Unpack in survival-kit order — bedroom first (bed frame, mattress, bedding, curtains) so you can sleep that night; kitchen next (cookware, tableware, condiments, cleaning supplies) so you can cook; living room last (sofa, coffee table, TV, router). Today is for unpacking only, no shopping: note whatever is missing on your phone and buy it in one trip tomorrow.
