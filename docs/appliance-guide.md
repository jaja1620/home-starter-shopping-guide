# 家电大件选购 / Major Appliances

覆盖五类必买大件：冰箱、洗衣机、烟灶套装、热水器、空调。每类给出预算区间、关键参数、实测机型与避坑提示，机型价格与 `docs/purchase-checklist.md` 第 1–5 项一致，均为 2026 年中国主流电商平台参考成交区间，非实时报价。

This guide covers the five must-buy major appliances: refrigerator, washing machine, hood-hob set, water heater and air conditioner. Each section gives a budget range, key specifications, tested models and pitfalls. Model prices match items 1–5 of `docs/purchase-checklist.md` and are reference prices on mainstream Chinese e-commerce platforms in 2026, not live quotes.

### 避坑提示 Pitfalls

- 别只看"总容积"，重点看冷藏室与冷冻室比例；冷冻室小于 80L 的机型，两人囤肉两周就满。Check the fridge-to-freezer ratio, not just total volume; under 80 L of freezer space fills up in two weeks for two people.
- 十字对开门看似大气，但单层搁板高度固定，放整只鸡或大汤锅会卡；三门与法式更适合两人家庭。Cross-door models look impressive but have fixed shelf heights that block whole chickens or stockpots; 3-door and French-door suit couples better.
- "零嵌/超薄"需要散热方式匹配，非嵌入式橱柜强行买超薄机，反而散热不足导致噪音大、耗电高。Slim or built-in models need matching ventilation; forcing a slim unit into a standard cabinet causes overheating, noise and higher power draw.
- 送货当天空载运行 2 小时再放食物，静置不足立即通电会损伤压缩机。Run it empty for 2 hours after delivery before loading food; powering up too soon can damage the compressor.

- 8kg 与 10kg 差价通常不足 300 元，但洗四件套时 8kg 需分两次；能放 10kg 就别省这一步。The gap between 8 kg and 10 kg is usually under CNY 300, but 8 kg needs two loads for a bedding set; take 10 kg when space allows.

- 别把洗衣机塞进封闭阳台暴晒位，紫外线会让控制面板与胶圈老化加速。Avoid sun-exposed enclosed balconies; UV ages the control panel and door seal quickly.

### 避坑提示 Pitfalls

- 只看风量不看静压是最大误区：低楼层风量 20m³/min 也够，高层若静压不足，做饭时烟道倒灌、别人家的味道全进屋。Judging airflow alone is the biggest mistake: 20 m³/min is fine on low floors, but on high floors insufficient static pressure lets the shared flue push smells back into your kitchen.
- 侧吸好看但占灶台上方空间，大蒸锅会顶；顶吸拢烟好但易碰头。按身高与常用锅具选。Side-draft hoods look better but block tall steamers; top-draft hoods capture smoke well but you may bump your head. Choose by height and cookware.
- 套装一定要"同一品牌同一渠道"下单，烟机与灶具分开买容易出现开孔不匹配、安装互相推诿。Order the set from one brand and one channel; buying hood and hob separately often leads to mismatched cut-outs and installers blaming each other.
- 公共烟道必须装止逆阀，且瓦工阶段就要预留烟道孔位置，贴完砖再打孔会破坏防水。A backflow damper is mandatory, and the flue hole must be reserved before tiling; drilling afterwards damages waterproofing.

### 避坑提示 Pitfalls

- 燃气热水器必须由燃气公司或有资质单位安装并通气验收，自行安装属于违规且无法通气。Gas water heaters must be installed and commissioned by the gas utility or a licensed installer; DIY installation is non-compliant and will not be connected.
- 零冷水机型需要预埋回水管，装修后再买只能退而求其次装"半零冷水"，效果打对折。Zero-cold-water models need a pre-laid return pipe; buying one after renovation forces a half-solution with much weaker results.
- 电热水器要确认电路承载：60L 3000W 需 4mm² 以上专线与单独空开，老房线路不足会跳闸。Confirm the electrical capacity for electric heaters: 60 L at 3,000 W needs a dedicated 4 mm² circuit and its own breaker; older wiring will trip.
- 燃气热水器不要装在浴室内，必须装在有窗通风处或阳台，烟管排至室外。Never install a gas heater inside the bathroom; it belongs in a ventilated area or on a balcony with the flue routed outdoors.

### 避坑提示 Pitfalls

- 报价通常不含高空作业费、支架费、加长铜管费与打孔费，两台机实际支出比标价多 300–600 元属正常。Quotes usually exclude high-altitude work, brackets, extra copper pipe and drilling; CNY 300–600 above list price for two units is normal.
- 空调孔必须在水电阶段打好并做内高外低斜度，后打孔易伤外墙保温且雨水倒灌。Drill holes during the electrical stage with a slight outward-downward slope; drilling later damages insulation and lets rain in.
- 别为省钱选三级能效：一台 1.5 匹三级比一级每年多耗电约 150–250 元，三年即抵消差价。Do not save with Grade 3: a 1.5 HP Grade-3 unit costs roughly CNY 150–250 more in electricity per year, offsetting the price gap in about three years.
- 装完必须试机 30 分钟以上，检查制冷、排水与是否有异响；冷凝水排水管要引到室外，别滴到楼下。Run each unit for at least 30 minutes after installation to check cooling, drainage and noise; route the condensate drain outdoors rather than onto the neighbour below.

## 安装排队与时间安排 Installation Queue and Timing

大家电安装排队通常 2–5 天，入住前 7 天必须全部预约完毕；具体每天做什么见 `docs/move-in-timeline.md`。建议把冰箱、洗衣机、热水器、烟灶、空调的报装分品牌一次性提交，避免多次上门导致重复等待。

Installation queues for major appliances run 2–5 days, so everything must be booked 7 days before move-in; see `docs/move-in-timeline.md` for the day-by-day plan. Submit installation requests per brand in one go for refrigerator, washing machine, water heater, hood-hob set and air conditioners to avoid repeated waiting.
