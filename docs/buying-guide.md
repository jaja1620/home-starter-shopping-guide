## 二、挑选原则 / 2. Selection Principles

### 1. 能效等级一级优先 / Prioritise Grade-1 Energy Efficiency

大家电优先选一级能效。一级与三级能效的差价通常 300–800 元，但冰箱与空调是一天 24 小时通电的设备，按日常使用强度，差价多在 1–2 年内由电费找回；热水器、洗衣机差价更小，回本更快。看标签上的"中国能效标识"，不要只看宣传页的"变频"字样——变频不等于一级。

Choose Grade-1 energy efficiency for major appliances. The price gap over Grade-3 is usually CNY 300–800, but refrigerators and air conditioners run 24 hours a day, so the difference is typically recovered through electricity bills within one to two years; water heaters and washing machines pay back even faster. Check the China Energy Label itself, not the inverter (变频) wording on the marketing page — inverter does not mean Grade-1.

### 2. 售后网点覆盖 / Service Network Coverage

下单前先查品牌在本地的售后网点与响应时效。冰箱、洗衣机、空调属于"坏了自己修不了"的品类，网点密度比参数更影响居住体验。全国性品牌（海尔、美的、格力、小天鹅、万和等）在多数城市可做到 24–48 小时上门；小众品牌价格便宜但配件周期长，若住所不在其重点服务区域，慎选。

Before ordering, check the brand's local service outlets and response time. Refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners are categories you cannot repair yourself, so outlet density affects daily life more than spec sheets do. National brands (Haier, Midea, GREE, LittleSwan, Vanward and others) usually reach your door within 24–48 hours in most cities; niche brands are cheaper but spare parts take longer — avoid them if you live outside their key service areas.

### 3. 尺寸预留 / Dimension Reservation

所有嵌入式或靠墙的大家电，都要先确认机器尺寸再定柜体与开孔：冰箱两侧与背部通常需留 5–10cm 散热缝，并确认开门方向可换；洗衣机要量机位宽度、深度与开门半径；烟机要按最大静压与公共烟道位置定安装高度；空调要预留内机上方 15cm、两侧 15cm 的空间。橱柜按错尺寸无法改，只能砸墙或换机。

For any built-in or wall-adjacent appliance, confirm the machine's dimensions before fixing cabinets and openings. Refrigerators normally need 5–10 cm of ventilation clearance at the sides and back, and you should confirm the door swing can be reversed. Measure the width, depth and door radius of the washing machine slot. Position the hood according to maximum static pressure and the shared flue. Leave 15 cm above and on both sides of an indoor air-conditioning unit. A cabinet built to the wrong size cannot be fixed — only demolished or replaced together with the appliance.

### 4. 噪音 / Noise

冰箱选标称噪音 ≤ 38dB 的机型，开放式厨房或客厅旁摆放尤其要注意；洗衣机脱水噪音看洗涤/脱水双参数，夜间洗衣优先选带"夜间洗"的；烟机大风量必然伴随高噪音，选带弱档且标注声压级的型号；空调睡眠模式噪音 ≤ 20dB 才算安静。参数表里没写噪音的机型，一律按"吵"预设。

Pick a refrigerator rated at ≤ 38 dB, especially in an open kitchen or next to the living room. For washing machines, check both wash and spin noise, and choose models with a night programme if you wash late. High airflow in a range hood always brings noise; choose models with a low setting and a stated sound-pressure level. An air conditioner is only quiet if its sleep mode is ≤ 20 dB. Any model that omits noise from its spec sheet should be assumed loud.

### 5. 质保年限 / Warranty Length

重点看三项：整机质保、核心部件质保、压缩机/电机等关键件质保。行业常见为整机 1 年、主要部件 3 年、压缩机 10 年，务必在下单页截图保存保修条款，并把发票或电子订单号登记进 `docs/warranty-register.md`。安装由品牌官方渠道完成，自行找师傅安装可能导致保修失效。

Look at three things: whole-unit warranty, core-component warranty, and warranty on key parts such as compressors and motors. Industry norms are 1 year for the unit, 3 years for major components and 10 years for compressors. Screenshot the warranty terms at checkout and log the invoice or order number in `docs/warranty-register.md`. Have installation done through the brand's official channel — self-arranged installation can void the warranty.

## 三、下单顺序建议 / 3. Ordering Sequence

### 原则一：先大件后小件 / Rule 1: Big Items Before Small Ones

大件的交期、安装排队与开孔尺寸都会影响后续所有工序，先锁定；小件单价低、型号多、随时可买，最后补。反过来做，最常见的后果是橱柜做完才发现冰箱塞不进去。

Big appliances have lead times, installation queues and opening dimensions that constrain everything downstream, so lock them first. Small items are cheap, widely available and can be bought any time — save them for last. Do it the other way round and the classic outcome is a finished cabinet that the refrigerator does not fit into.

### 原则二：先隐蔽工程后软装 / Rule 2: Hidden Works Before Soft Furnishings

水电改造、烟道孔、空调孔、燃气管道属于"改完就难回头"的隐蔽工程，必须在墙面与吊顶封闭前完成定位；窗帘、地毯、挂画、绿植等软装放在最后，避免施工粉尘污染与磕碰。

Electrical and plumbing modification, flue holes, air-conditioner holes and gas piping are hidden works that are hard to redo once closed up; they must be positioned before walls and ceilings are sealed. Curtains, rugs, artwork and plants come last, so they are not covered in construction dust or damaged.

## 四、省钱时机 / 4. When to Save Money

### 1. 大促节点 / Major Promotion Windows

618 与双 11 是大家电价格的两个低点，通常比日常价低 8%–15%；年货节、818、品牌周年庆属于次级节点。策略是提前 2–4 周把机型加进购物车、记录历史价格，促销开始当天再下单，避免"先涨后降"。空调避开 6–7 月安装高峰，3–4 月与 9–10 月议价空间更大。

618 and Double 11 are the two annual lows for major appliances, typically 8%–15% below normal prices; New Year sales, 818 and brand anniversaries are secondary windows. Add models to your cart 2–4 weeks ahead, record historical prices, and place the order on the first day of the promotion to avoid inflated "discounts". For air conditioners, avoid the June–July installation peak; March–April and September–October leave more room to negotiate.

### 2. 以旧换新 / Trade-In

主流平台与品牌均支持以旧换新，旧机折价通常 100–500 元，且包含免费上门拆机与回收。即便手上没有旧机，也可以在二手平台买一台几十元的废旧家电参与活动，多数情况下仍划算；注意确认"折价是否可与补贴叠加"。

Major platforms and brands all run trade-in programmes, with old units typically valued at CNY 100–500 including free removal and recycling. Even without an old appliance you can often buy a scrap unit cheaply on a second-hand platform and still come out ahead — but confirm whether the trade-in value stacks with subsidies.

### 3. 国家补贴约 15% / National Subsidy, About 15%

一级能效家电可参与国家补贴，常见比例为成交价的 15% 左右、单件设有上限，需在指定平台实名领取并在下单时勾选。叠加顺序通常是：先算活动价，再减补贴，再减以旧换新，最后才是实际支付。补贴名额与品类每年调整，下单前以平台页面公示为准。

Grade-1 energy-efficient appliances qualify for the national subsidy, commonly around 15% of the transaction price with a per-item cap, claimed on designated platforms with real-name verification and applied at checkout. The usual stacking order is: promotion price first, then subsidy, then trade-in, and only then the amount actually paid. Quotas and eligible categories change every year; follow the platform's published rules on the day you order.
